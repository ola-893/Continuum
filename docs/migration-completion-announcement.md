## Getting Started

### For Existing Users

Your assets and streams have been automatically migrated to Tezos. Here's how to access them:

**Step 1: Connect Your Tezos Wallet**
- Visit https://continuum.protocol
- Click "Connect Wallet"
- Choose Temple, Kukai, or Umami wallet
- Approve the connection

**Step 2: View Your Assets**
- Navigate to "Dashboard"
- All your NFTs and streams are ready
- Balances are updating in real-time

**Step 3: Start Using**
- Claim your yield
- Transfer assets
- Create new streams
- Everything works just like before!

### For New Users

Welcome to Continuum Protocol! Here's how to get started:

**Step 1: Get a Tezos Wallet**
- Download Temple Wallet (browser extension)
- Or use Kukai (web wallet)
- Or install Umami (desktop/mobile)

**Step 2: Get Some XTZ**
- Purchase XTZ from an exchange
- Transfer to your wallet
- You'll need XTZ for gas fees

**Step 3: Connect and Explore**
- Visit https://continuum.protocol
- Connect your wallet
- Explore available assets
- Start earning yield!
